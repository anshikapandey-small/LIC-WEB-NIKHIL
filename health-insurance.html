Health Insurance: common questions

Family floater or individual plans?

A floater covers the whole family under one sum insured, while individual plans give each person their own. I’ll compare both based on your family’s ages.

Is my employer’s cover enough?

Group cover usually ends when you change or leave your job, so a personal policy is a safer base.

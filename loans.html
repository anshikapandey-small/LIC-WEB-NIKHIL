Loans: common questions

What kinds of loans can you help with?

I help with both secured loans, such as those backed by property, and unsecured loans. Which one suits you depends on the amount, purpose and your profile.

Do you decide the interest rate?

No. Rates, fees and approval are decided by the lender. I help you compare options, prepare documents and follow the application through.

What documents are usually needed?

Typically ID and address proof, income proof and bank statements. The exact list depends on the lender and loan type, and I’ll confirm it before you apply.
